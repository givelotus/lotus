Limits:
- Slot-byte counters describe constructed slots, excluding unused chunk capacity,
  allocator overhead, transaction bodies and RSS. They are not total memory.
- Last-owner destruction is synchronous; batching bounds mutex hold time, not
  the complete release pause. Cold hashing regressed in the 500k microbenchmark.
- Deferred overlay adds still flush under mempool locks before structural pin.
- Depleted sketches may scan pinned membership outside capture locks. Separate
  snapshots can refill independently; threshold hash ties can exceed 256 candidates.
- MinHash is public/grindable and advisory only; it cannot authorize membership,
  admission, voting, or finality. Scope5 wire OFFER is version 2 and includes the
  sketch; this is a documented private wire delta, not a consensus hash change.
- Chronik-enabled full build, long-duration RSS profiling and 500k all-peer node
  completion were not performed here. Two-node55-transaction acceptance and
  microbenchmarks must not be described as a 500k all-peer completion result.
